Cookie Policy

What cookies we use, why we use them and how to manage your preferences.

Effective date: 1 January 2026
Last updated: 26 May 2026
Controller: Tax Advisory & Accounting Albania — albtaa.com

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language or settings, making your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

2. Cookies We Use

We keep our cookie use to an absolute minimum. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, social media pixels or any third-party analytics that profile you individually.

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function correctly. They cannot be disabled.

Name Purpose Duration Type
albtaa_lang Stores your language preference (EN or IT) so the correct language is shown on each page load. Until cleared Essential

2.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality. You may disable them, but some features may not work as intended.

Name Purpose Duration Type
albtaa_consent Records that you have acknowledged this Cookie Policy, so the notice is not shown on every visit. 12 months Functional

2.3 Analytics & Advertising

We do not currently use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) or advertising networks. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated and, where required, your consent will be requested before any such cookies are placed.

3. localStorage (Not Cookies)

Our website uses the browser's localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store your language preference (albtaa_lang) and consent acknowledgement (albtaa_consent). localStorage data is stored entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

4. How to Manage Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Below are links to instructions for the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this and many other websites you visit. Clearing your browser's localStorage will reset your language preference to the default.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for operational, legal or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about our use of cookies or this Cookie Policy, please contact us via our contact form. For broader privacy concerns, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
