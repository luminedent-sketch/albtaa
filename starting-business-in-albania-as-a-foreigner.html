FOREIGN OWNERSHIP IN ALBANIA

Can Foreigners Start a Business in Albania? Yes — Fully and Legally.

Albanian law places no restriction on foreign ownership. Whether you're a UK citizen, a US company, or an entrepreneur based anywhere else in the world, you can own 100% of an Albanian company — no local partner, no local shareholder, no local director required. Non-EU citizens hold the same company ownership rights as Albanian and EU citizens.

You also don't need to relocate. Company registration, tax registration and VAT setup can all be completed remotely through a power of attorney — the entire foundation of your business can be in place before you ever set foot in Albania, if you choose to visit at all.

What foreign founders actually need isn't permission — it's someone who manages the process correctly from the first filing to the first tax return. That's where ALBTAA comes in.

WHY FOREIGN FOUNDERS CHOOSE ALBANIA

A Practical Base for an International Business.

Albania sits within a short flight of most major European cities and shares time zones with Central Europe, which matters if you're running client calls or managing a distributed team. Operating costs — office space, salaries, professional services — remain considerably lower than in Western Europe, without the setup complexity found in some other jurisdictions.

Company formation itself is fast by regional standards, and the corporate tax framework includes a 0% corporate tax rate for companies with annual gross revenue under 14,000,000 ALL, under Albanian Law No. 29/2023 — a meaningful advantage for early-stage and small businesses. None of this replaces proper planning, but it does mean the administrative side of starting a business here doesn't have to be the hard part.

AFTER YOUR COMPANY IS REGISTERED

Incorporation Is the Start. Compliance Is What Keeps It Running.

A registered company with no accounting behind it is a liability, not an asset. Once your business is active, Albanian law requires regular bookkeeping and periodic tax filings — and if you're registered for VAT, recurring VAT declarations on top of that. Payroll, if you hire locally, brings its own set of obligations.

This is why ALBTAA doesn't treat registration as the finish line. We work with foreign founders as a long-term accounting and compliance partner — the same team that opens your company is the one that keeps it compliant a year, or five years, later. One point of contact, instead of switching providers every time a new obligation comes up.
