VAT NUMBER IN ALBANIA

What You Need to Know, in Short.

Albania doesn't have a separate "VAT number" application: your NIPT is issued automatically when your company is registered with the QKB and already serves as your tax code and, when applicable, your VAT number — no additional filing required. The standard VAT rate is 20%, with reduced rates for certain sectors like hotel accommodation. The entire process, from signing the power of attorney to an active NIPT on e-Albania, takes 3–5 business days.

VAT: WHEN YOU PAY IT AND WHEN YOU DON'T

In Most Cases, You Pay Less VAT Than You Think.

Many foreign founders fall into one of two favorable situations: below 10,000,000 ALL in annual revenue (roughly €108,000), VAT is not due — your NIPT still stays fully active and operational. And if you invoice consulting, software or digital services to European clients, that counts as a "service export," exempt from VAT under double-taxation treaties between Albania and several EU countries.

Everything You Need to Know About
the VAT Number in Albania

The NIPT is Albania's unique tax identification number, assigned automatically when a company is registered with the QKB. It functions as a tax code and, for businesses required to register for VAT, also as the VAT identifier — making it the Albanian equivalent of a VAT number.

The standard VAT rate in Albania is 20%. Reduced rates apply to certain sectors such as hotel accommodation and specific food products, to be verified case by case in your free quote.

VAT registration becomes mandatory once you exceed the annual threshold set by Albanian law; below that threshold, voluntary registration is still possible, which is useful if you invoice abroad or want to reclaim VAT on purchases. We confirm your exact situation in your free quote.
