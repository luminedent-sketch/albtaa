6 REASONS TO CHOOSE ALBANIA

Albania in 2026 —
The Numbers That Matter

0%
Corporate Tax Until 2029

Companies with revenue under 14,000,000 ALL (≈€140,000/year) pay zero corporate income tax — guaranteed by Albanian Law No. 29/2023 through 31 December 2029. Above threshold: 15% flat. Compare to Italy's 24%, UK's 25%, Germany's ~30%.

€1
Minimum Share Capital

An Albanian SHPK requires just 100 ALL (≈€1) minimum capital. No capital lock-up. No statutory audit requirement for small companies. No local partner required — one foreign individual can be sole founder and shareholder.

3–5
Days to Register — Fully Remote

Albania's digital business registration system (QKB/NBC) processes applications fast. We submit everything online. You never need to visit Albania — the entire process is done remotely via Power of Attorney.

15%
Flat CIT Above €140K — Still Europe's Lowest

Even above the 0% threshold, Albania's flat 15% CIT rate is among the most competitive in Europe. For context: Italy 24%, Germany ~30%, France 25%, UK 25%, Netherlands 25.8%.

8%
Dividend Withholding Tax

Albania's dividend withholding tax is just 8% — compared to 26% in Italy or 25% in Germany. Albania has 40+ double taxation treaties (DTT) that may further reduce this rate depending on your home country.

EU
Official EU Candidate Country

Albania is an official EU accession candidate with strong institutional reforms underway. Albanian companies benefit from EU trade agreements, proximity to European markets and a fast-growing economy with increasing foreign investment.
