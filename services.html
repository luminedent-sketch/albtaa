03 — TAX ADVISORY ALBANIA

Tax Advisory & 0% CIT Planning
for Foreign Investors

Strategic tax counsel to help your Albanian company operate at the lowest possible effective tax rate — starting with a full analysis of eligibility for Albania's 0% corporate income tax under Law No. 29/2023, valid through 31 December 2029.

KEY TAX ADVANTAGE
0% Corporate Income Tax — Law No. 29/2023

Any Albanian-registered entity with annual gross revenue below 14,000,000 ALL (≈€140,000) pays zero corporate income tax, codified until 31 December 2029. Above the threshold, the standard flat CIT of 15% applies — still one of Europe's lowest.
