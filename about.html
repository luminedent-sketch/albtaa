WHAT WE DO

We Register Albanian Companies
for Foreign Investors — That's Our Focus

ALBTAA is not a generalist accounting firm. We have built our entire operation around one service: getting foreign entrepreneurs, investors and companies registered and operational in Albania as fast, cleanly and affordably as possible.

Every client we work with is a foreign entrepreneur or company entering Albania for the first time. We know every step of the process, every document requirement and every common mistake — so you don't have to learn any of it.

WHY CLIENTS CHOOSE ALBTAA

What Makes Us Different
from Other Albanian Firms

We Speak Your Language — Natively

English and Italian fluency — not via translation software. Your advisor communicates directly in your language, understands your context and explains Albanian law in terms that make sense to you.

100% Remote — You Never Need to Visit Albania

The entire registration process is done remotely via Power of Attorney. We handle everything in Tirana — you send us your documents from wherever you are in the world.

Fixed Fee From €250 — No Hidden Costs

You receive a fixed-fee quote before committing. Our €250 fee covers articles of association, NBC filing, tax registration and registered address. Notary and translation costs, if needed, are billed separately at cost — confirmed upfront in your quote.

One Advisor — One Point of Contact

You work with one named advisor throughout your entire registration — not a call centre, not rotating staff. Same person from first contact to company certificate.

Response Within 24 Hours — Always

We respond to all inquiries within one business day. Active clients get direct WhatsApp access to their advisor — no waiting, no email queues.

Licensed & Certified in Albania

Our advisors hold Albanian professional certifications and are registered with the relevant Albanian professional bodies. You deal with certified professionals — not informal advisors or middlemen.
