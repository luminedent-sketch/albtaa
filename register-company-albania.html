SHPK or Person Fizik — which is right for you?

Two structures cover the vast majority of foreign investors registering in Albania. The right choice depends on your income level, liability exposure, and whether you invoice corporate clients.

✦ Often the simpler choice

SHPK

Shoqëri me Përgjegjësi të Kufizuar

  • Separate legal entity — personal assets protected
  • No minimum share capital required
  • 100% foreign ownership permitted
  • 15% CIT on profits (0% if turnover below ~€140k)
  • 8% dividend tax on profit distributions
  • 20% VAT on taxable supplies above threshold
  • Mandatory monthly and annual accounting
  • Best for: companies, investors, liability-sensitive activities
Sole Trader

Person Fizik

Sole Trader / Self-Employed

  • Registered in owner's name — simpler structure
  • No liability separation from personal assets
  • 0% income tax if annual income below ~14M ALL
  • Lower accounting and compliance costs
  • Ideal for freelancers, consultants, remote workers
  • VAT threshold same as SHPK (~€100k turnover)
  • Less credibility with some larger corporate clients
  • Best for: solo operators, digital nomads, consultants

The registration process — step by step

Structure selection & engagement

Document preparation

Day 1–4 · Remote

QKB registration via eAlbania

We file with the National Business Center (QKB) via eAlbania using your Power of Attorney. Registration is free of government fees.

Day 3–5 · Remote · 1–3 day processing

NIPT issuance & DPT registration

Your NIPT (Albanian tax ID) is assigned by QKB upon registration. We simultaneously register your company with the General Directorate of Taxes.

Day 5–7 · Automatic upon QKB approval

We open your eTatime account and configure Fiskalizimi (Albania's mandatory real-time e-invoicing system) before your first sale.

Day 7–12 · Remote

Bank account opening

Most Albanian banks require the administrator to visit a branch in person at least once for KYC. We prepare all documents in advance so your visit takes no more than one morning. Some banks accept remote KYC for EU nationals — we advise case by case.
