WHY ALBANIA, WHY NOW

A Market Institutional Investors Are Actively Watching

Albania's investment case has shifted in recent years. What used to be a niche, cost-driven decision is now backed by regulatory alignment, legal protections, and a growing base of international corporate presence.

Institutional interest is real, not speculative. German enterprises have been prominent participants in Albania-focused forums such as the German-Albanian Business Conference, organised with the involvement of DIHK (the German Chamber of Industry and Commerce) — with companies including BMW Group, Deutsche Telekom, TUI and Lidl represented in discussions on the Albanian market. GIZ, the German development agency, maintains an active programme in Albania.

On the American side, AmCham Albania has operated as an accredited affiliate of the U.S. Chamber of Commerce since 2003, reflecting a sustained institutional relationship between American business interests and the Albanian market.

ALBTAA is an independent Albanian tax advisory and accounting firm — we are not affiliated with the companies or organisations named above. We reference them to illustrate the level of institutional interest in the Albanian market, not to imply any relationship with ALBTAA.

FREE ECONOMIC ZONE

TEDA Tirana — A Structural Advantage for Qualifying Investors

Albania operates government-backed Free Economic Zones, with TEDA Tirana among the most established. For investors whose activity qualifies — manufacturing, logistics, processing, and certain industrial or export-oriented operations — the zone offers a materially different cost and administrative structure than a standard onshore registration:

Reduced Tax Exposure
Qualifying zone operations benefit from reduced corporate tax treatment relative to standard onshore rates.
Customs Exemptions
Equipment and machinery imported for zone-based operations can qualify for customs duty exemptions.
Streamlined Administration
Zone-based operations are typically supported by a single-window administrative process, reducing bureaucratic overhead.
TALENT & COST

Skilled Workforce, Western-European Work Ethic, Fraction of the Cost

Albania's proximity to Italy and the wider Adriatic — combined with a young, multilingual workforce — makes it a genuine nearshore alternative for companies used to hiring in Germany, Austria, or the broader DACH region, at a fraction of the fully-loaded cost.

Multilingual Talent Pool
English and Italian proficiency are widespread; German-language skills are a growing segment of the graduate talent pool.
Competitive Labour Costs
Salaries and operating costs sit meaningfully below Western European levels, without the compliance risk of less-regulated markets.
One Hour From Rome, Two From Frankfurt
Direct flight connectivity to major EU business hubs keeps oversight and travel logistics practical, not theoretical.
