RESIDENCE PERMIT IN ALBANIA

Owning a Company Doesn't Require a Permit. Living There Does.

This surprises most founders: registering and owning 100% of an Albanian company carries no residence requirement at all. You can manage the business entirely from abroad, sign documents remotely, and never set foot in Albania if that's what suits you. A residence permit only becomes relevant the moment you actually want to live there — whether full-time, or for extended stretches beyond what a tourist visa allows.

If that's your plan, the good news is that your company registration already does most of the heavy lifting. Albania's residence system, modernized under Law No. 79/2021, ties the permit directly to your business activity rather than requiring a separate immigration process from scratch.

HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

One Card, Not Two Separate Processes.

Albania replaced its older, multi-tier permit system with the Unique Permit (Leje Unike) — a single biometric card that combines residence and work authorization in one document, applied for through the e-Albania digital portal. There's no separate work permit to chase down afterward; it's built into the same application.

For most founders running an LLC or registered as a sole trader, the relevant route is the self-employment Unique Permit — the standard path for small business owners, consultants, and service providers who want to reside in Albania based on their own registered business, rather than being hired by someone else's company.

VALIDITY AND RENEWAL

From First Approval to Permanent Residence.

The initial permit is typically issued for one year, and renewable for as long as your registered business stays active — the permit is tied to the business being real and ongoing, not a one-time formality. Requirements generally include proof of your company registration, a business bank account with adequate funds, and documentation about what the business actually does.

Maintain continuous legal residence for five years, and you become eligible to apply for permanent residence — at that point, your right to stay in Albania is no longer tied to renewing the permit year after year. It's a clear, predictable path rather than an open-ended process, which is exactly why we walk through the full timeline with founders before they commit to it.
